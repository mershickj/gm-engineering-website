FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Our engineering team is ready to help.

Contact our team
Which states does GM Engineering work in?

GM Engineering is headquartered in Aralvaimozhi, Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu, and has executed and contributed to projects across Tamil Nadu, Madhya Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Maharashtra.

What size of solar and wind projects do you handle?

We handle everything from rooftop solar systems of 0.10 kW to 100 kW and above, through to utility-scale work such as a 200 MW solar plant erection and commissioning and 50 to 75 MW wind power projects. Our executed and ongoing scope exceeds 350 MW.

Do you handle high-voltage substation and transmission line works?

Yes. We execute 33 kV and 132 kV bay construction, pooling substation equipment erection, 33 kV transmission lines with CEIG liaison, unitised substations, ABT metering and SCADA automation.

Can you help HT industrial consumers reduce electricity costs through open access?

Yes. We facilitate bilateral power procurement from solar, wind and small hydro generators, handle DISCOM and regulatory liaison, and draft wheeling, LTOA, MTOA and short-term open access agreements.

What rooftop solar investment models do you offer?

We offer a CAPEX model, where the client owns the system and receives full power savings, and a RESCO / OPEX model, with zero upfront capital and power supplied at a discounted tariff under a power purchase agreement.

Planning a solar, wind or substation project?

Whether it is a MW-scale plant, electrical infrastructure, open access or forecasting, our engineering team is ready to assist.
