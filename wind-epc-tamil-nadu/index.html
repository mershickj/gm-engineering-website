Wind Power Project Execution

Working in Tamil Nadu's wind country

Aralvaimozhi sits in one of India's best-known wind regions. The Aralvaimozhi pass channels strong monsoon winds onto the Muppandal wind farms of Kanyakumari District, and wind development continues north through Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi districts.

From here our team has executed installation, electrical and construction works on wind projects across Tamil Nadu, on Suzlon, Gamesa, Siemens Gamesa, Pioneer WINCON and SPRNG machines, including construction works at Gamesa's Kayathar site.

We work as a main contractor or as a subcontractor to OEMs and EPC partners, and we can also deliver the substation, evacuation line and scheduling work that keeps a wind farm connected and compliant.

Contact our team
Which wind turbine platforms have you worked on?

Our wind project record includes Suzlon (2.1 MW machines and a 50 MW project), Gamesa (a 75 MW project and Kayathar site construction works), Siemens Gamesa 2.5 MW, Pioneer WINCON 750 kW and SPRNG 3 MW machines.

Do you build the 33 kV lines and substations that evacuate wind power?

Yes. We execute power transformer erection, 33 kV and 132 kV bay construction, pooling substation equipment erection and 33 kV Dog / Panther line works with CEIG liaison.

Do you provide wind generation forecasting and scheduling?

Yes. We provide CERC and SERC-compliant day-ahead, intraday and week-ahead forecasts with SLDC telemetry and scheduling, helping wind generators lower DSM deviation penalties.
