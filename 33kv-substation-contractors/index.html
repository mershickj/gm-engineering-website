High-Voltage Works

Specialised team for HV infrastructure

Our electrical team executes high-voltage works for the power and heavy industrial sectors. On renewable projects we build the switchyard and evacuation network; for industrial and HT consumers we supply and install unitised substations and the metering needed for grid compliance.

Projects include switchyard bay works and equipment installation for a 15 MW solar plant, transformer erection for a 50 MW wind project and 33 kV single-circuit Dog / Panther line works.

Every job follows our safety culture of PPE, electrical isolation and safe work practices, with compliance to CEIG, SLDC, CERC / SERC and CEA standards.
